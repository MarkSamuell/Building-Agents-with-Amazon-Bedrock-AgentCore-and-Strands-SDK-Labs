# Horizon Travel — Official Policies and Terms

Document Version: 2026.1 | Effective Date: 1 January 2026

---

## 1. Flight Booking and Ticketing

### 1.1 Booking Confirmation
All flight bookings are confirmed upon receipt of full payment. A confirmation email with a booking reference (format: BK-XXXX) will be sent within 15 minutes of payment. Tickets are issued electronically (e-tickets); no physical tickets are issued.

### 1.2 Name Changes
Name corrections (minor spelling errors, up to 3 characters) are permitted free of charge up to 24 hours before departure. Full name transfers are not permitted on any ticket type.

### 1.3 Fare Classes
Horizon Travel operates three fare classes:
- **Economy Lite** — no changes, no refunds, one cabin bag only
- **Economy Flex** — one free change, refundable with a 15% administration fee
- **Business** — unlimited changes, fully refundable, lounge access, priority boarding

---

## 2. Cancellation and Refund Policy

### 2.1 Cancellation Timeframes and Fees

| Time Before Departure | Economy Lite | Economy Flex | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| More than 7 days | No refund | 85% refund | Full refund |
| 48 hours to 7 days | No refund | 70% refund | Full refund |
| 24 to 48 hours | No refund | 50% refund | 90% refund |
| Less than 24 hours | No refund | No refund | 75% refund |

All refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

### 2.2 Horizon Travel-Initiated Cancellations
If Horizon Travel cancels a flight, all passengers receive a full refund regardless of fare class, plus a voucher worth 20% of the original ticket value for a future booking.

### 2.3 No-Show Policy
Passengers who do not check in and do not cancel at least 2 hours before departure forfeit the full ticket value on Economy Lite and Economy Flex fares. Business fare passengers receive a 50% refund.

---

## 3. Baggage Policy

### 3.1 Cabin Baggage
All fare classes include one personal item (max 40 cm × 30 cm × 15 cm, max 7 kg). Economy Lite includes only a personal item; no full-size cabin bag. Economy Flex and Business include one cabin bag (max 55 cm × 40 cm × 20 cm, max 10 kg) in addition to the personal item.

### 3.2 Checked Baggage Allowance

| Fare Class | Included Checked Bags | Max Weight per Bag |
|---|---|---|
| Economy Lite | 0 (must purchase) | 23 kg |
| Economy Flex | 1 bag | 23 kg |
| Business | 2 bags | 32 kg each |

### 3.3 Additional and Excess Baggage Fees
- Extra checked bag (pre-booked): USD 45 per bag per flight
- Extra checked bag (at airport): USD 75 per bag per flight
- Excess weight (23–32 kg on Economy): USD 30 per kg
- Excess weight (32–45 kg, Business only): USD 20 per kg
- Oversized items (over 158 cm linear): USD 80 per item per flight

### 3.4 Prohibited Items
The following are prohibited in all baggage: lithium batteries over 160 Wh, firearms without prior written approval, aerosols over 500 ml, and any items banned under IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations.

---

## 4. Check-In Policy

### 4.1 Online Check-In
Online check-in opens 48 hours before departure and closes 2 hours before departure for international flights, 90 minutes before departure for domestic flights. Seat selection is free during online check-in for Economy Flex and Business; USD 12–25 per seat for Economy Lite.

### 4.2 Airport Check-In
Airport check-in desks open 3 hours before departure and close 60 minutes before departure for international flights (90 minutes for flights to/from the USA, Canada, and Australia). Arriving after desk closure may result in denied boarding with no refund.

### 4.3 Boarding
Boarding begins 45 minutes before departure and closes 15 minutes before departure. Business class and Platinum loyalty members board first, followed by families with young children, then Economy Flex, then Economy Lite.

---

## 5. Flight Changes

### 5.1 Voluntary Changes
- **Economy Lite:** No changes permitted after booking.
- **Economy Flex:** One free change (same route); subsequent changes: USD 35 each. Fare difference applies.
- **Business:** Unlimited changes; fare difference applies only.

### 5.2 Involuntary Changes (Horizon Travel-Initiated)
If Horizon Travel changes a flight by more than 3 hours, passengers may: (a) accept the new schedule, (b) rebook on the next available flight at no charge, or (c) receive a full refund.

## 6. Travel Insurance

### 6.1 Standard Travel Insurance
Available at booking for USD 29 per person per trip (up to 14 days). Covers:
- Trip cancellation for covered reasons: up to USD 3,000
- Medical emergency and evacuation: up to USD 50,000
- Delayed or lost baggage: up to USD 500 (after 6-hour delay)
- Flight delay compensation: USD 50 per 4-hour delay (max USD 200)

### 6.2 Premium Travel Insurance
Available at booking for USD 59 per person per trip (up to 14 days). All Standard coverage, plus:
- Cancel for Any Reason (CFAR): 75% refund of non-refundable costs
- Medical emergency and evacuation: up to USD 250,000
- Delayed or lost baggage: up to USD 2,000
- Trip interruption: up to USD 5,000
- Pre-existing medical conditions: covered if purchased within 14 days of initial trip deposit

### 6.3 Insurance Claims
Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the insured event. Documentation required: booking confirmation, receipts, medical reports (if applicable), and police report (for theft). Claims are processed within 15 business days.

## 7. Loyalty Programme — Horizon Rewards

### 7.1 Membership Tiers

| Tier | Points Required | Annual Renewal |
|---|---|---|
| Silver | 0 – 999 points | No minimum required |
| Gold | 1,000 – 4,999 points | 1,000 points per year |
| Platinum | 5,000+ points | 3,000 points per year |

### 7.2 Earning Points
- Economy Lite flights: 1 point per USD 1 spent
- Economy Flex flights: 1.5 points per USD 1 spent
- Business flights: 3 points per USD 1 spent
- Hotel bookings through Horizon Travel: 2 points per USD 1 spent
- Travel insurance purchase: 1 point per USD 1 spent
- Partner spending (car hire, transfers): 1 point per USD 1 spent

### 7.3 Redeeming Points
- Minimum redemption: 500 points (= USD 5 discount)
- 100 points = USD 1 discount on any Horizon Travel booking
- Points redemption cap: 50% of the booking value
- Points are valid for 24 months from the date earned
- Points cannot be combined with promotional discount codes

### 7.4 Tier Benefits

**Silver:**
- Access to the Horizon Rewards member portal
- Birthday bonus: 200 extra points

**Gold:**
- Free seat selection (standard seats) at booking
- One free additional checked bag per flight
- 10% discount on travel insurance
- Access to priority check-in queue
- Birthday bonus: 500 extra points

**Platinum:**
- Free seat selection (including extra legroom) at booking
- Two free additional checked bags per flight
- 15% discount on all bookings
- Priority boarding
- Access to Horizon Lounge at partner airports
- Free same-day flight change (once per year)
- Dedicated Platinum customer service line (24/7)
- Birthday bonus: 1,000 extra points

### 7.5 Points Expiry
Points expire 24 months after the earning date. Any account activity (flight booking, redemption, partner earn) resets the clock on all points in the account. Platinum members receive a 6-month extension on all points annually.

## 8. Special Assistance and Accessibility

Horizon Travel welcomes passengers requiring special assistance. Please notify us at least 48 hours before departure for:
- Wheelchair assistance (ramp, aisle chair, or self-propelling)
- Travelling with a guide or assistance dog (48-hour notice, documentation required)
- Unaccompanied minor service (available for children aged 5–15, USD 60 per leg)
- Medical oxygen (72-hour notice, medical certificate required)
- Stretcher service (72-hour notice, medical clearance required)

## 9. Passenger Rights and Complaints

### 9.1 Flight Disruption Compensation
In the event of a delay or cancellation caused by Horizon Travel (not force majeure):
- 3–4 hour delay: meal voucher (USD 15)
- Over 4 hours: meal voucher (USD 25) + 1,000 bonus loyalty points
- Over 8 hours on long-haul: hotel accommodation if overnight

### 9.2 Denied Boarding (Oversold Flights)
Voluntary volunteers receive: full refund or rebooking + USD 300 travel credit. Involuntary denied boarding: USD 600 compensation + full refund or next available flight.

### 9.3 Complaints Process
Complaints must be submitted in writing (complaints@horizontravel.com or via the website) within 14 days of the event. Horizon Travel will acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 28 calendar days.
